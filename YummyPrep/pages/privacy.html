YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how YummyPrep collects, uses and protects information provided by customers while using our website and services.

01

Introduction

YummyPrep is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our customers. When you use our website, create an account, customize a meal or place an order, certain information may be collected to provide our services.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly when using YummyPrep.

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Account login information
  • Order and meal customization information
  • Contact form messages and customer feedback
03

How We Use Your Information

Customer information may be used for purposes related to providing and improving YummyPrep services.

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing and preparing orders
  • Delivering meals to the selected address
  • Displaying order history and tracking information
  • Responding to customer questions and support requests
  • Improving our meals, website and customer experience
04

Payment Information

When customers place an order, YummyPrep may collect information related to the selected meals, customized ingredients, quantities, delivery preferences and total order amount.

This information is used to prepare, manage and deliver customer orders correctly.

05

Data Security

YummyPrep takes reasonable steps to protect your personal information. Account passwords are stored in an encrypted (hashed) form and we do not store card numbers or card security codes on our system.

Our website uses a session cookie to keep you logged in while you browse. You can end your session at any time by choosing Log Out.

06

Your Choices

You can view and update your name, phone number and delivery address at any time from your Profile page. If you would like your account to be removed, please contact us and we will assist you.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your details are used only to prepare, deliver and support your orders.

07

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach us through the Contact page.
