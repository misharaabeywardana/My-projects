YUMMYPREP GUIDELINES

Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions explain the guidelines for using the YummyPrep website, creating an account, customizing meals and placing orders.

01

Using YummyPrep

By using the YummyPrep website, customers agree to use the website and its services responsibly and according to these Terms & Conditions.

Customers should provide accurate information when creating an account, placing an order or contacting YummyPrep.

02

Customer Accounts

Customers may create an account to access features such as their profile, order history and order tracking.

Customers are responsible for providing correct account information and keeping their login information secure.

03

Meal Orders

Customers can select available YummyPrep meals and add them to their shopping cart before proceeding to checkout.

Customers should review the selected products, quantities and order total before confirming an order.

04

Customized Meals

YummyPrep allows customers to create meals by selecting available bases, proteins, vegetables, toppings, sauces and other customization options.

Customers are responsible for reviewing their selected ingredients before adding a customized meal to the cart.

05

Prices

Meal prices are displayed on the YummyPrep website. The final amount may depend on the selected meal, quantity and customization options.

YummyPrep may update meal prices or promotional offers when necessary.

06

Delivery

A delivery charge of Rs. 250 is added to customer orders.

Customers may choose their preferred available delivery date during checkout. Orders may be scheduled for delivery within the day or for the following day depending on the customer's selected delivery preference.

Customers should provide an accurate delivery address and contact number to help ensure successful delivery.

07

Order Changes & Cancellations

Customers should carefully review their order before confirming it.

Requests to change or cancel an order may depend on whether preparation of the order has already started. Customers should contact YummyPrep as soon as possible if a change is required.

08

Food & Ingredient Information

YummyPrep provides meal descriptions and ingredient information to help customers understand the available meal options.

Customers should review the ingredients selected for their meals before placing an order.

09

Promotions

YummyPrep may provide promotional offers, discounts or special deals from time to time.

Promotions may be available for a limited period and may be subject to specific conditions.

10

Website Availability

YummyPrep aims to keep the website and its services available and functioning correctly. However, temporary interruptions may occur due to maintenance, technical issues or updates.

11

Privacy

Information provided through the YummyPrep website is handled according to our Privacy Policy.
