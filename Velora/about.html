OUR STORY

Crafting Memorable Stays Since Day One

Velora began with a simple idea: travel should feel effortless and every stay should feel personal. From cosy mountain cabins to beachfront resorts, we partner with hand-picked hotels across Sri Lanka to bring you comfortable rooms, fair prices and genuine hospitality.

Today, thousands of travellers trust Velora to plan their getaways, family holidays and business trips. We're proud to keep things simple, transparent and centred around the traveller.
